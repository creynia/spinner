# E5: the universal curve (H3) over E3, E4 and E6

Serves plan.md §4 E5 and §2 H3. HANDOFF.md §7 wave 2. Inputs: every `docs/research/results/*.json`
as of 48f1049, including `e4-straights35.json`. Also uses tiny `onCap: 'stop'` twins run for this
brief (not committed). Each is the committed spec with `onCap` set to `stop`, the same seed
20261031 and `--sessions 2000`, so it covers the first 2,000 sessions of the ledger run. There are
twelve: E3a L5–L10, E3b ×6, and the four recovering ×2 controls of E4. They had zero rejected or
adjusted rounds, and every residual |z| was ≤ 1.3.

**Definitions (one convention for every row).**
- B = cap loss / τ̄, where τ̄ is the mean net of a winning cycle. So B is the bankroll measured in
  cycle *profits*.
- x = p·S/B, where p is the number of winning cycles per spin (p_eff for E6).
- k = qB/(1 − q). D6's form is P(no cap) = e^(−k·x), which equals e^(−p·S·q/(1 − q)). B cancels
  out, so D6's form is H1's law restated. By the cycle's EV identity k = 1 + 0.027·(wager per
  cycle)/(τ̄(1 − q)) ≥ 1, so e^(−x) is a ceiling.
- E3: Martingale red, $5 unit, p = 18/37, q = (19/37)^L, B = 2^L − 1, τ̄ = $5.
- E4: τ̄ and the cap loss are computed from each spec's amounts. The rounding bumps make τ̄ slightly
  above net₀. **Note:** E4's `meta.B` is in level-0 *stakes*, so the aggregator's x for E4 rows is
  too large by the payout m (×8 on the corner). The x below is corrected.
- E6: meta values. For E6, the cap loss is −min(net).

"Never capped" is 1 − (sessions ended by `strategy:cap-*`)/N, taken from the stop row or its twin.
A reset row with no twin is marked "P(ahead) only".

**Excluded from the fit:**
- `recovers: false` rows (two-dozens-x2, straights25-x2, straights35-x2), as briefs/E4 says. Their
  τ̄ is not defined.
- Cash-out and trailing rows (`d-*`). They end early, so their never-capped share is censored, not
  a 500-spin share.
- E6 screen rows whose ladder also ran at full size, so the same sessions are not counted twice.
  The full row pools 3 seeds × 10,000.

The fit uses 53 ladders. "·" marks a row whose share is already counted elsewhere: a stop twin of a
reset row, or a screen duplicated by a full row.

## 5. Conclusions (for plan §7)

1. **H3 holds as a ceiling.** None of the 53 ladders (E3, E4 including 35 straight-ups, E6) beats e^(−x) by any amount. On average they sit 6 points below it; 35 straight-ups sit 45 points below.
2. **D6's form e^(−x·qB/(1−q)) = e^(−p·S·q/(1−q)) is the point prediction.** RMS residual 0.8 points, RMS z 1.5. The three z > 2 rows are long ladders (L 20–53), where the exact run-length DP closes the gap (single coverage: RMS z 0.72, max 1.5). No residual pattern by coverage or by bet type remains.
3. **Coverage is not a lever beyond bankroll in cycle profits.** At equal B and x, layouts tie within sampling error (b3/b4, b5/b6, b7/b8). Layout only enters k = qB/(1−q), which runs from 1.14 (one street stacked) to 1.27 (Martingale red), 1.37 (two dozens) and 1.58 (widening to 33 numbers).
4. **Best-case gain from layout at equal B:** +3.3 points of P(no cap) over red-scaled at the leaders' x = 0.41, and never more than +7 (the unreachable ceiling). In dollars: under $70 of median at fixed C, or +11 % on the largest target that stays positive. Widening is the wrong direction: it raises k.
5. **What moves the median horizon** is S½ = ln2·B/(k·p) = ln2·C/(k·p·τ̄). In order: the dollars committed per dollar of profit per spin (bankroll over base stake corrected by payout, B ≈ C/(u·m)), then k ≥ 1, then the stopping rule (cash-out turns the median into the target, with P ≈ (1−q)^W).
6. **The median check:** "median ≈ x·B level-0 profits" (= p·S·τ̄) overstates the measured median: the measured value runs 2–15 % below it (mean 6.6 %), because the median session is the poorest survivor. τ̄ × the (1 − 1/(2P0)) quantile of Bin(S, p) lands within −3 % to +6 % (mean |err| 1.3 %). Every positive median comes with a negative mean (−$110 to −$602) and a CVaR 5 % of −$1,275 to −$5,115.
7. **Verdict for E6:** stop searching layouts. Search B/u (smaller base stake or a larger committed bankroll) and cash-out or trailing rules. Use the narrowest matched-growth stack for its k. Tooling note: give E4's `meta.B` in cycle profits, or have the aggregator divide by m.
